JobTailor AI

An AI copilot that tailors your resume and cover letter to any job posting, in one click, right from your browser.

What it does

JobTailor AI is a Chrome extension. Open any job listing — LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, and more — and it reads the posting, then rewrites your resume and cover letter to match it: relevant experience surfaced first, key terminology mirrored naturally, every fact still truthful to your real background.

  • Detects the job title, company, and description automatically
  • Generates a tailored resume, laid out to match your existing format, auto-fit to one page
  • Generates a matching, professional cover letter
  • Downloads both as ready-to-send PDFs
  • Keeps a running log of every application you've tailored
